## Dear Prasasti A.R. Dajanty

<br/>
You're the person I want to dance in the kitchen with,

Kiss you while showering,

Make breakfast while I washing the dishes,

Go out at night randomly,

Hear you say "munchies" out of the blue,

Laugh and make memories with.

<br/>
Maybe I do fall in love with you since the beginning,.. 

just like u said, what is love anyway?

Well.. if this is love,
<br/><br/>
```
I'll choose to stay in love with you, over and over again.
```
