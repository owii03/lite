## Dear Prasasti A.R. Dajanty

<br/>
Being with you..

Like I've been knocking on life's front door,

Suddenly you crack it open—and there's warmth.

<br/>
You speak in ways that rearrange my spine,

Make me sit straighter, listen harder,

Remember that respect isn't a rule—it's a rhythm,

And you've been dancing in it since birth.

<br/>
You surprise me.

Not with flash, but with follow-through.

With care so consistent

It makes me question what I used to call love.

<br/>
You make me check myself—not 'cause you asked,

But your presence is a mirror

And your patience is a lesson

I didn't know I needed.

<br/>
When you look at me,

I feel like something more than just seen—I feel known.

You listen like I matter.

You move through this world

Like it's lucky to hold you—and I can’t help but agree.

<br/>
Awestruck,

Grateful,

Still catching up to the privilege

<b>Of calling you mine.</b>
