## Dear Prasasti A.R. Dajanty

<br/>
I don't want the polished version of you—

I want the messy one.

The one who doubts,

The one who cries,

The one who's still figuring it out.

I want the one who shows up even when it's hard.

<br/>
I know you—

The cracked parts,

The tired heart,

The quiet fears you carry in your chest.

At your worst. At your mess. At your most human.

<br/>
I'm not here for the highlight reel—

I'm here for the rest.

The late nights.

The quiet fights.

The healing that takes time.

I'm not in this for a moment.

<b>I'm in this for lifetime.</b>
