## Dear Prasasti A.R. Dajanty

<br/>
<b>You're my iris.</b>

Not the flower you give on Sundays,

But the truth you whisper on Mondays,

Not the flower you post on birthdays,

But the steady, quiet kind of love.

<br/>
The kind that shows up.

That stays.

That holds you down when life gets loud.

<br/>
<b>You're my iris.</b>

The eye of the storm where I feel still,

The part of the song that gives me chills.

You’re the hush between heartbeats,

The part of the night where the stars lean in just to hear you breathe.
