## Dear Prasasti A.R. Dajanty

<br/>
I never got to be soft like this before.

Never got to laugh in the middle of sentences or mess up my words and not apologize for it.

Never got to be clumsy with affection, light with my love, loud with my joy.

<br/>
Around you,

I become someone I thought the world had buried.

I don't have to be perfect around you.

<b>And that feels like a kind of freedom I didn't know I'd live long enough to find.</b>
