## Dear Prasasti A.R. Dajanty

<br/>
<b>Let me be the one.</b>

The one you tell jokes to—jokes you can’t finish without laughing.

The one who knows your favorite meals and exactly how they should be served.

The one who drives with you at night to random places, without caring about the time.

The one who dances and sings along with you to any genre of music you feel like listening to.

The one who plays along with your word games and finishes your sentences before you even say them.

<br/>
<b>Let me be the one.</b>

The one you turn to whenever you have a problem.

The one who may not always have a solution, but never stops offering you my shoulder to rest your head on.

The one you share your wildest fantasies and intrusive thoughts with—without judgment, just a gentle “sigh.”

The one you can spend hours cuddling with, who listens as you say, “What if I’m gone? Will you be sad?” or “You don’t love me,” and still keeps reassuring you.

<br/>
<b>Let me be the one—to do life with you.</b>
