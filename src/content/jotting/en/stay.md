## Dear Prasasti A.R. Dajanty

<br/>
I wish you knew how terrifying it is for me when we fight.

How one harsh word from you has me spiraling into a thousand worst-case scenarios.

How every argument feels like a ticking clock, counting down to the moment you walk away.

I know I'm stubborn. I know I hurt you. Betrayed you.

<br/>
I wish you'd pull me into your arms mid-argument and say,

"I'm mad, but I'm not leaving."

</br>
You said, how it made you feel last night, "hard to handle"

But no. You are not hard to handle. You are not a burden.

<br/>
Instead..

I fear that I'm too much.

Fear that I'm not enough.

<br/>
I'm sorry.

For the hurting. The anxiety. The betrayment. 

But I really wish you always stay.

<br/>
So stay.
<b>Please, just... stay.</b>
