## Dear my Dearest

<br/>
I have been turning this moment over in my head for weeks now, trying to find the right words. Something worthy of you. But here is the truth—nothing I write could ever fully hold the way I feel about you. Still, I will try.
<br/><br/>
We have shared so many ordinary moments that have somehow turned into my favorite memories. And honestly? You still surprise me—like how you somehow manage to be intimidating and adorable at the same time. It is kind of unfair. But I forgive you.
<br/><br/>
I love how you do that tiny victory dance when you eat something that makes your soul happy—like Immanuel's food especially when you eat the Klapertart and chocolate pudding. Not to forget the KFC cream soup, your ultimate comfort in a chaotic world. I love that you absolutely refuse to coexist peacefully with cockroach.
I love the way you hate pickles inside your burger, and how you prefer Nestle more than Aqua as your mineral water.I love the way your eyes soften during night rides, how the city lights seem to calm your storms when words fall short. 
<br/><br/>
I love the unplanned car ride where suddenly we were at PIK or Puncak (sounds normal), but also somewhere in the wood (Rembember Bali and Jogja road? I think we really love forest.)
<br/><br/>
I remember that night—Jakarta, 1 a.m., and nothing in our way but empty roads and the shared mission of finding that one ice cream flavor you were craving like your life depended on it. Most people would’ve called it ridiculous. I called it magic. Because it was with you.
<br/><br/>
And maybe that’s what I want for the rest of my life:
Late-night drives, quiet laughs, and the stubborn pursuit of little joys.
Like finding new foods you can eat that aren’t spicy (don’t worry, I’ll take the heat for us both).
Like building a life that feels like that 1 a.m. ride—unexpected, warm, a little wild, and deeply ours.
<br/><br/>
I’m not promising perfection. I’ll probably still forget something important and pretend I didn't (badly). I'll still tease you when you do that dramatic sigh over something minor. Not just for the fairy tale, but for the grocery runs, the sleepy mornings, the tough days, and the slow dances in the kitchen when we’re too tired to go out. I'll also be the one holding your hand through every hard day and making sure you never, ever have to face cockroach alone.
<br/><br/>
I vow to choose you every day.

To hold your hand through joy and storms.

To be your partner, your safe place, and your biggest fan.
<br/><br/>
I want to build a life with you that feels like us—equal parts chaos and calm, ridiculous jokes and quiet moments, wild dreams and warm hugs.
<br/><br/>
So here I am, not on one knee just yet (I’ll save it for later), but with a question that’s been echoing in my chest for some time now:
Will you hold your ground beside me—as we build, defy, and conquer together?
<br/><br/><br/>
Your future

Always,

<b>Gloria C.M.</b>
