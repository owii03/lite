## Dear Prasasti A.R. Dajanty

<br/>
I have never known how to care for another

<b>Until you.</b>

<br/>
I remember

The way your voice shifts when sorrow stirs within you,

The way your fingers search for steady ground when you are anxious,

The way your tears fall, silent and brave, upon your cheeks.

<br/>
I will not love you in fragments.

<b>I will love you whole.</b>

<br/>
I will love you in a way that says,

<b>"You are safe here."</b>

In a way that hums,

<b>"You no longer have to bear this alone."</b>

<br/>
You have fought for so long, with no one beside you.

Let me be the one who stands with you now.

Let me be the one who stays.
