## Dear Prasasti A.R. Dajanty

<br/>
You—

You take away the need to perform.

<br/>
With you,

I don't have to shrink my love

Or second-guess if it's too much.

Because with you, too much is just enough.

<br/>
With you,

I don't fold.

I don't hide.

You see me—

The real me— and somehow…

That feels like a revolution.

<br/>
You're not a dream—

You're the realest thing I know.

You don’t light up the sky

You light up the room

When you walk through the door

<br/>
And I remember why

<b>I choose you.</b>

Every. Single. Time.

Every version.

Flawed. Unfiltered. Unfolding.
