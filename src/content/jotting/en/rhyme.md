## My Heart Sing for You

<br/>
You smiled before you said your name. 

And somehow I still knew the frame of every word you hadn't said. 

Like finding notes inside my head.

You laugh and I could hum along as if I'd always known your song.

The melody just fit my chest like something old.

I loved the best.

I knew your song before we met. 

Each rise and fall I can't forget. 

Like a wind that sings through open blinds.

You've always lived between the lines.

<br/>
No lyrics carved, no sheet to read. 

But still you play just what I need. 

Somewhere in me, 

You've always been the song I knew way back when. 

<br/>
We walked without a place to be, just following the harmony.

The city moved, but not like us.

We dance without the need to rush.

I told you things I'd never say.

You didn't look the other way.

You just listen with your eyes and sang your truth in no disguise.

I knew your song before the star.

The way you breathe.

The way you part, the way you hold the silent space.

You fit the cords I can't replace. 

<br/>
So sing with me until we're gone.

Even if the notes go wrong, you are the song I didn't write.

But somehow knew my whole damn life.
