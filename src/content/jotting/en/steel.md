## Dear Prasasti A.R. Dajanty

<br/>
From where I stand,

You're not sweet.

You're fire wrapped in steel —fierce, unwavering.

You stand like armor —not defensive, but devoted.

<br/>
You speak when silence would be easier,

You defend me like my pain's your own.

You carry memories like weapons —

Sharpened by every time someone crossed you.

<br/>
You're loud when it matters,

Gentle when I need softness,

Utterly relentless when it's us against the world.

You make my heart race — yet somehow, I find peace in your presence.

<br/>
I'd be a fool to ever let you go.

I'd trade my pride if that’s what it takes to make you stay.

I'd rewrite the clock, bend every rule —just to keep you close.

<br/>
You're exactly right.

You're what fits.

You're what I long for.

You're mine.

And I am endlessly,

<b>Unquestionably, yours.</b>
