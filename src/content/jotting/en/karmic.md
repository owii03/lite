## Dear Prasasti A.R. Dajanty

<br/>
You once told me there was someone.

A fleeting glance, a moment suspended in time.

An instant connection, like your soul knew theirs

From some place it couldn’t name.

<br/>
You called it karmic.

A meeting made not to last—but to teach.

<br/>
And yet,

when I look at you, there is no lesson.

Only a memory that hasn’t happened yet.

It doesn’t feel like knowing you—

It feels like remembering.

<br/>
The curve of your smile,

The way you speak with your hands,

The quiet notes of your laughter—They feel like home.

As if we were once two halves of the same breath.

<br/>
And now,

I find myself holding onto a truth I cannot explain:

That I have known you before.

That I have loved you

In another time,

A different place—

Some other existence.
