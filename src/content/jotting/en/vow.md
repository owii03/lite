## I do Solemnly Vow to Prasasti Anggita Restu Dajanty

<br/>
<b>I take you.</b>

Not just in these moments of joy,

but in the moments of breaking.

Not just in the laughter,

but also in times when words fail,

and the silence filled with quiet tears.

I take you as you are,

as God is shaping you to be.

And still, I choose you

over and over again for all my days.

<br/>
<b>I vow.</b>

To love you with a love that is patient,

A love that choose grace over anger,

Understanding over pride.

A love that doesn’t waver,

Doesn’t turn away when life weighs down on you,

A love that stays.

<br/>
<b>I vow.</b>

To remind you how precious you are

When world makes you question it,

To speak hope when the doubt is too loud,

To hold your hand even when I don’t fully understand,

But hold it still.

<br/>
<b>I vow.</b>

Not to rely on my own strength,

But to kneel in surrender

To fight for you,

To pray with you,

To live for you,

And above all,

To love you,

For a lifetime.
