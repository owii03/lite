## Dear Prasasti A.R. Dajanty

<br/>
Do you know, a woman is never afraid of her favorite man?

And do you know a man is always afraid of his favorite woman? 

But why is it like that?

<br/>
Because for a woman, her man is her home and her peace...

And no one is ever afraid of their home-where they feel safe and loved. 

<br/>
But a man... He's afraid of his favorite woman because he never wants to lose her,

And he silently loves how she controls him with her eyes.

<br/>
True that I am not a man,

But still, it applies to me as well.
